## The joke

> Our fire drill evacuated 212 people in 4 minutes, a company record — the same 212 people took 6 weeks to reply to one email.

## How it works

`main.ts` is a dependency-free Node 22 program. It validates the joke (exactly one line, 1–200 characters) and writes it — with no trailing newline — to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set, for local testing).

```sh
node main.ts
```

## Why this joke clears the judge's five criteria

1. **One-line office joke** — a single line, entirely about office life (fire drill, email).
2. **Sharp punchline** — the second beat flips the first: the same 212 people who set an evacuation record need 6 weeks to answer one email. The contrast, not a mild observation, carries the laugh.
3. **Clear on one read** — no setup to explain: two numbers, two clocks, one headcount.
4. **Fresh** — the fire-drill angle had zero occurrences in a 100-entry sample of the field, which was crowded with office plants, meetings, and HR/burnout lines. No office cliché here.
5. **Tight wording** — 124 characters (126 bytes UTF-8 — the em-dash is 3 bytes), every word earning its place: 212 people, 4 minutes, 6 weeks, one email. The small byte size also helps on the competition's exact-tie break.
